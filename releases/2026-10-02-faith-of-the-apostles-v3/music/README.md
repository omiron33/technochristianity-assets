# The Faith of the Apostles · v3

Web media for the original Orthodox song and lyric film [The Faith of the Apostles](https://technochristianity.com/music/?work=faith-of-the-apostles).

- `faith-of-the-apostles.mp4`: a full-length 1920 × 1080, 30 fps H.264/AAC delivery copy transcoded directly from the approved v3 film master. V3 revises the Creed sequence with fire around the disputed addition. The film is also [on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9gm3WmhYlE).
- `posters/faith-of-the-apostles.jpg`: a frame from v3 at 84.86 seconds.
- `manifest.json`: exact byte lengths, SHA-256 hashes, and master provenance.

The original [48 kHz, 16-bit stereo WAV](../../2026-10-02-faith-of-the-apostles/music/faith-of-the-apostles.wav) and measured lyric timing are unchanged. The [film source repository](https://github.com/omiron33/faith-of-the-apostles) holds the scene code and lyrics. The original recording is [on Suno](https://suno.com/song/2a8faba8-f258-473a-bd04-994c498f22bf). This is an original song, not a complete Scripture chapter or a liturgical text.

This dated release is immutable. Previous releases remain available in their original directories. Source-code licenses do not automatically license the song recording or other separately credited assets.
