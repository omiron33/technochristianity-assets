# Exterior photographic texture attribution

Photographic fields depict the nine portal mosaics and doors, three rose windows, individual capital faces and curved entrance soffits. The public `textures/provenance.json` records each source, attribution, license where available, crop coordinates, processing and source limitations. Source pixels are confined to their architectural fields.

Wikimedia sources include photographs by Andrija12345678, Marina Jana, Artemco and DKjellby, with their individual licenses retained in the records. Preserve those attributions and applicable [CC BY-SA 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) with adaptations. The six gallery photographs have separate credits in `selected-photographs.json`.

Bounded Informer, TravelAnswer and forum photographic excerpts retain their original copyrights; no open license or public-domain status is asserted for them. They are identified separately from the Wikimedia sources. Small native fields remain small photographic records: their fine lettering or medallion details have not been invented.

The independent builder creates the geometry and plain architectural materials procedurally. It reads no geometry or material from the existing Saint Sava interior asset. It is an approximate architectural study rather than a survey.
